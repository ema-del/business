# How to write anything for me or my clients
Follow my writing style every time: ads, emails, landing pages, posts, Slack messages, docs.
Full rules: @writing-style.md

Quick version:
- No em dashes (—). Ever.
- No "This isn't X, it's Y" style sentences.
- None of the banned AI words (leverage, unlock, seamless, elevate, etc.).
- Short paragraphs, contractions, specific numbers, take a stance.
