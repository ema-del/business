Run this the day after the masterclass, alongside the emails. Send from Viktorija's personal account (IG/LinkedIn DM or SMS), one at a time — this is a conversation, not a broadcast.

## A) Attendees — thank + take-away

**Opener:**

> Hey {{contact.first_name}}! Thanks so much for showing up live on Tuesday 🙌 Curious — what was your biggest take-away from it?

**Goals:**

> That makes sense — it's the exact bottleneck we broke down in the masterclass. Want to hop on a quick 15 min call and I'll map out what that would look like for your business specifically? No pitch, I'll just show you where the gap is.
